How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

No sign-up, no upload, no design skills. If you can pick a colour, you can do this.

01

Bring your QR

Drop in a screenshot of your Fonepay QR, or paste any link and we will make one from scratch. Your existing QR is read on your own device.

02

Make it yours

Add your logo, your colours, a gradient, rounded dots, your business name and a caption. Or hit a preset and skip the fiddling.

03

Check, then download

The result is scanned back before you can save it. Download as PNG, JPG, WebP at 3x, or as an SVG that stays sharp on a shop banner.

Why you can trust it

A pretty QR that doesn't pay you is worse than a plain one.

This is money, so the tool refuses to hand you something it has not checked. Every time you change a colour, a shape or the logo size, the design is decoded again from the pixels, exactly as a customer's phone would read it.

It re-scans your design

The rendered QR is read back with the same decoder a phone camera uses, not simply assumed to be correct.

It compares the payload

The decoded result must match your original byte for byte. The same account, the same amount rules, the same link.

Download stays locked

If the two do not match, the button will not let you export. A broken or wrong QR cannot leave this page.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Will the branded QR still pay my account?

Yes, and the tool proves it rather than promising it. The design you download has been decoded back from its own pixels and matched against your original payload. If anything differs, the download is blocked. That said, always test-scan with your own phone before printing a hundred copies.

Is my QR uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no server to upload it to. The page loads, and everything after that happens in your browser using your own device. You can turn off your internet after the page loads and the tool still works.

Does it work with QRs from any bank or wallet?

It works with any standard payment QR, including Fonepay QRs from Nepali banks and wallets, because it preserves whatever data is already encoded rather than rebuilding it. It also handles plain links, Wi-Fi codes and anything else a QR can carry.

How large can the logo be?

The slider stops at about a quarter of the code, because beyond roughly 30% even high error correction starts to fail. The tool uses the strongest correction level so a centre logo is safe, and the re-scan check catches anything that goes too far.

Which format should I download?

SVG for anything printed, since it stays sharp at any size, from a business card to a shop banner. PNG for screens and social posts. JPG and WebP are there for when a platform insists on them. Raster exports render at 3x so they hold up in print.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no limit on how many you make. The code is open source under the MIT licence, so you can read exactly what it does or run your own copy.

Is this made by Fonepay?

No. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Fonepay Payment Service Ltd. or any bank. It re-renders a QR you already have and never moves money.
