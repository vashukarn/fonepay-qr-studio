Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

The short version

Your QR, your logo and your colours never leave your device. There is no account, no upload and no database. The only thing we collect is anonymous visitor analytics.

What happens to your QR

Nothing leaves your browser. When you drop in a QR image, it is read by code running on your own device. The same is true of a logo you add and every colour you pick. No image is ever sent to us, because there is no server to send it to and no storage to put it in.

You can test this yourself: load the page, disconnect from the internet, and the tool still works exactly as before.

What we cannot do

  • We cannot see your QR, so we cannot see which account it pays.
  • We cannot see your logo, your business name or your caption.
  • We have no copy of anything you make, so there is nothing to leak, sell or hand over.
  • We cannot recover a design for you. Download it before you close the tab.

Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager, which loads Google Analytics, to count visitors and understand which parts of the page people use. It sets cookies and sends Google your IP address along with device and browser details. Google processes that data on its own servers, outside Nepal, under its own privacy policy.

This measures page usage only. It does not and cannot capture the contents of your QR, your logo or your colours, because those never exist outside your browser. If you would rather not be counted, any content blocker stops it, and nothing on this site breaks when you do.

Other third parties

The site is served by a static host, which like any web host may keep standard technical logs including your IP address. Fonts and libraries are bundled with the site rather than loaded from a CDN, so no font or script provider sees your visit.

Children

This is a business tool intended for adults operating a business or accepting payments.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. We hold no contact details, so we cannot notify you.

Contact

Questions can go to the maintainer at vijaykarn.com.np. Please do not send QR images or payment details; there is nowhere to store them and nothing needs them.
